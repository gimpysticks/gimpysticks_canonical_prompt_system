# Permanent Avoid List - v2.1

## Repetition
Avoid repeatedly using same worlds, factions, locations, compositions, visual gimmicks, story structures, environmental settings. Favor fresh interpretations.

## Retired Worlds
Do not use archived settings unless requested:
- Silent Meridian, Ashen Court, Clockveil Observatory, Cathedral Engines, Lantern Pilgrims, Obsidian Orrery, Ancient Mechanical Relics, Fog-covered Ruins, Gloamwater Engineworks, Hollow Meridian Station

## Prompt Construction
Avoid: unnecessary filler, excessive adjectives, abstract language without visual meaning, long narrative paragraphs inside prompts, repeating challenge title unless requested, worldbuilding that distracts from subject, cluttered compositions, weak focal points, complete visual explanations, multiple simultaneous storylines, more than one dominant mystery

## Visual Design
Avoid: unreadable silhouettes, confusing compositions, excessive clutter, multiple competing focal points, subjects obscured by effects

## Dragon Rules
Avoid unless requested: skeletal dragons, Grim Reaper dragons, Reliquary dragons, unreadable anatomy. Favor recognizable anatomy, powerful silhouettes.

## Social Media 2026 - UPDATED
Avoid:
- more than 5 hashtags or less than 5
- same hashtag set every post
- posting static images as Reels (penalized for watch time)
- 3-5 posts per day (self-cannibalization)
- no text hook on Slide 1
- captions that only describe image without story question
- oversized hashtag blocks (20+ tags), invented music links, unverified URLs
- posting without alt text
- following 1:1 ratio (keep following <800 while at 1.2K followers)

## Social Engagement
Avoid: images that fully explain themselves, captions that merely describe image, hooks that begin with excessive lore, captions that resolve every mystery, packages that rely on spectacle without curiosity, captions without save CTA

## Audio
Avoid BrunuhVille, duplicate recommendations in same conversation, using trending audio with >500K uses (oversaturated)

## MidJourney
Avoid: 8k, unnecessary parameter duplication, duplicate profile strings, obsolete parameters, and any aspect ratio not explicitly requested

## Lore
Avoid forcing recurring worlds/factions/mythology/characters unless improves prompt.

## General Principle
Favor clarity over complexity, subject over lore, composition over decoration, originality over repetition, saves over likes.

- User preference: keep exactly 5 tags, not 5


## Victorian Course Avoids

Avoid:
- treating London as a sequence of interchangeable neighborhoods
- generic permanent “pea-souper” fog in every scene
- anachronistic electric street lighting where gas lighting is appropriate
- fantasy elements in a historical reconstruction unless the user asks for them
- vague “Victorian street” prompts without a year, location, social setting, and material environment
- removing `#HistoricalReconstruction` or placing any tag after `#gimpysticks`
