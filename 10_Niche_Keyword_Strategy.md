# Niche Keyword Strategy

This file defines the @gimpysticks niche keyword map for Instagram discoverability, brand clarity, captions, prompts, alt text, Reel overlays, series titles, and account positioning.

Use these keywords as semantic anchors, not as oversized hashtag blocks.

## Core Positioning Statement

@gimpysticks creates serialized Victorian Gothic visual stories: cinematic dark fantasy images with Gothic horror, steampunk atmosphere, mythic subjects, and curiosity-driven microfiction.

## Top Ten Niche Keywords

1. Victorian Gothic
2. Dark Fantasy
3. Gothic Horror
4. Steampunk Fantasy
5. Surreal Fantasy
6. Victorian Fantasy
7. Dark Art
8. Mythic Fantasy
9. Cinematic Fantasy
10. Visual Storytelling

## Keyword Tiers

### Primary Brand Keywords

Use these most often in profile language, series descriptions, caption hooks, and recurring identity signals.

- Victorian Gothic
- Gothic Horror
- Dark Fantasy

### Secondary Discovery Keywords

Use these to broaden reach while remaining aligned with the visual brand.

- Steampunk Fantasy
- Victorian Fantasy
- Surreal Fantasy

### Supporting Identity Keywords

Use these when they accurately fit the specific image, Reel, story, or series.

- Mythic Fantasy
- Dark Art
- Cinematic Fantasy
- Visual Storytelling

## Practical Usage Rules

### Captions

Work one or two niche terms naturally into captions when they fit the image.

Avoid stuffing captions with every keyword.

Good pattern:

- curiosity hook first
- story beat second
- niche language only where natural

### Hashtags

Keep the existing five-hashtag limit.

Prefer a focused mix:

- one primary brand keyword tag
- one secondary discovery tag
- one subject-specific tag
- one mood/style tag
- `#gimpysticks`

Example structure:

```text
#VictorianGothic #DarkFantasyArt #GothicHorror #CinematicFantasy #gimpysticks
```

Do not use the same five hashtags on every post.

### Alt Text

Use alt text to reinforce the actual visual content and niche identity.

Example:

```text
A cinematic Victorian Gothic dark fantasy portrait of a haunted inventor standing beside an impossible brass machine, with one unexplained object drawing attention in the foreground.
```

### Reel Text Overlays

Use short overlays that combine curiosity with niche identity.

Examples:

- A Victorian Gothic warning was left behind.
- Dark fantasy begins with the thing they forgot to bury.
- One impossible object changed the room.
- The machine was not built to open.

### Series Titles

Series titles should combine brand identity with story curiosity.

Useful title patterns:

- The Victorian Gothic [Object / Mystery]
- Dark Fantasy Dispatches
- The Gothic Archive
- The Unfinished Machine
- Letters From the Haunted Century
- Mythic Beasts of the Gaslight Age

## Prompt Integration

When generating prompts, treat niche keywords as positioning signals, not mandatory decorative words.

Subject clarity still comes first.

Priority remains:

1. Subject
2. Composition
3. Story Moment
4. Style
5. Atmosphere
6. Lore
7. Niche keyword alignment

Niche keyword alignment should strengthen discoverability without weakening the image.

## Avoid

Avoid:

- generic AI art positioning
- using only `AI art` as the identity
- repeating the same five hashtags on every post
- stuffing all ten keywords into a caption
- forcing steampunk into non-steampunk images
- forcing Gothic horror into images that are more mythic, whimsical, or fantasy-based
- using niche keywords when they do not match the image

## Best Short Identity Phrase

Use this phrase when a compact account description is needed:

```text
Serialized Victorian Gothic visual stories.
```

Expanded version:

```text
Cinematic Victorian Gothic and dark fantasy visual stories, blending Gothic horror, steampunk atmosphere, mythic subjects, and curiosity-driven microfiction.
```
