# Midjourney Control Profile: Mannerist Anatomy & Controlled Elongation

**Aesthetic Objective:** Stylized, elongated Gothic Mannerism with rigid skeletal structure and bounded limb proportions. Prevents rubbery, over-extended, or melting extremities.

---

### Core Anatomy Tokens (Positive Weighting)
* **Controlled Proportions:** `graceful elongated silhouette`, `mannerist proportions`, `sculptural anatomy`, `natural limb length`, `proportional forearm ratio`, `bounded limb reach`
* **Joint & Bone Integrity:** `articulated elbow joint`, `rigid skeletal frame`, `visible defined clavicle`, `structured wrist joint`, `firm muscular anatomy`, `structural bone tension`
* **Hands & Extremities:** `accurately scaled five-finger hands`, `natural hand size relative to forearm`, `delicate grounded fingers`, `proportional palm`

---

### Structural Negative Prompts (`--no`)
Add these to suppress extreme stretching and noodle-arm artifacts without flattening the artistic style:

```text
--no rubbery limbs, infinitely long arms, dislocated shoulders, noodle arms, hyper-extended forearms, melting fingers, extra finger joints, disproportionately long limbs, warped hands, missing joints, mutated wrists
